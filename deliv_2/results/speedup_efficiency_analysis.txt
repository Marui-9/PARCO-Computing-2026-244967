====================================================================================================
PARALLEL SPEEDUP AND EFFICIENCY ANALYSIS
MPI-Based Sparse Matrix-Vector Multiplication (SpMV)
====================================================================================================

METHODOLOGY:
- Baseline: 1-node, 48 cores per node (1 MPI rank × 48 OpenMP threads)
- Speedup: time_1node / time_N_nodes (relative to 1-node execution time)
- Efficiency: (speedup / num_nodes) × 100%
- 11 sparse matrices tested (rows: 36K to 5M, densities: 0.0001 to 1.5%)
- 6 MPI communication modes evaluated
- 30 iterations per matrix/configuration/node count

====================================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS AND INSIGHTS
====================================================================================================

1. SPEEDUP SCALING:
   - 2 nodes:  ~1.67x speedup (83.4% efficiency) - Good scaling for MPI overhead
   - 4 nodes:  ~3.18x speedup (79.6% efficiency) - Near-linear scaling
   - 8 nodes:  ~4.11x speedup (51.3% efficiency) - Sub-linear due to communication overhead
   - Maximum observed speedup: 4.35x (MPI_Bcast+Reduce at 8 nodes)

2. CONFIGURATION PERFORMANCE:
   - Best:  Isend/Irecv (2.84x avg speedup)
   - Worst: Pipelined (2.76x avg speedup)
   - Difference: 3.0%

3. EFFICIENCY ANALYSIS:
   - Peak efficiency at 3 nodes: 96.5% (excellent scaling)
   - Efficiency decreases with node count (communication dominates)
   - At 8 nodes: 51.3% efficiency (communication overhead limits speedup)

4. COMMUNICATION OVERHEAD:
   - 1 node:  ~0.22 ms (baseline)
   - 2 nodes: ~0.34 ms (56% increase - minimal)
   - 8 nodes: ~0.066 ms average (faster due to parallelization, but efficiency drops)

5. RECOMMENDATIONS:
   - For small-scale (2-4 nodes): All configurations perform similarly well
   - For larger scale (8 nodes): Choose communication-efficient configurations
   - Communication efficiency differences are minimal (<3%)
   - Scaling limits (~50% efficiency at 8 nodes) suggest communication becomes dominant
